# Field validation plan (Bengaluru, 12–14 Nov 2026)

*Status: **planned.** Results will be added here after the visits, anonymised: no names, phone numbers, shop names or exact locations. The interview guides are in [field-kit.md](field-kit.md).*

## Who we will meet

| Group | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Retail jewellers who make custom or wedding orders | 6–8 | The core buyer |
| Wholesalers or manufacturers who issue gold to many karigars | 1–2 | A bigger buyer, with many karigars at once |
| Karigars, met away from their jeweller | 6–8 | The core user; fairness |

| JanAI Café, CSC or similar operator (by phone) | 1 | The assisted path and its fees |

**Where:** Avenue Road, Chickpet and Nagarthpet. **In all:** about 10 jewellers and 10 karigars, plus 6 others.

## What we expect, and what would change our minds
Each hypothesis has a starting estimate (with its basis) and a pass mark fixed **before** the visits.

| # | Hypothesis | Starting estimate and basis | Holds if | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Disputes or unexplained shortfalls are common | Common: the statement says disputes follow, and absconding cases of ₹98 lakh–₹2.66 crore have been reported | ≥ 6 of 10 jewellers recall one in the last six months | After 14 Nov |
| H2 | It costs real money or time | Normal measurement error alone is about ±105 mg on one 46 g return ([worked example](problem-and-landscape.md#a-worked-example-why-honest-people-still-fight)) | Median ≥ 1 g a month **or** ≥ 3 hours a week; at least one absconding story per market | After 14 Nov |
| H3 | Today's record is one-sided | The jeweller's notebook is the record (statement) | ≥ 7 of 10 karigars hold no copy they control | After 14 Nov |
| H4 | Karigars want a shared record and will confirm it by voice or tap | Untested: our riskiest assumption | ≥ 6 of 10 say yes **and** ≥ 8 of 10 restate the weight and date after hearing a mock voice receipt | After 14 Nov |
| H5 | The channels work | WhatsApp and UPI are universal among migrant workers | ≥ 8 of 10 karigars use WhatsApp; ≥ 6 of 10 send voice notes | After 14 Nov |
| H6 | Capture can be fast | Untested | Today's issue entry takes ≥ 30 s, and scale displays photograph cleanly | After 14 Nov |
| H7 | Deadlines slip | Wedding-season pressure (statement) | ≥ 5 of 10 jewellers had a late custom order last season | After 14 Nov |
| H8 | Karigars wait to be paid | Weeks: accounts are squared at month end (statement) | Median ≥ 14 days from delivery to payment | After 14 Nov |
| H9 | Someone will pay and commit | Untested | ≥ 4 of 10 jewellers agree to a December pilot; ≥ 3 of 10 call ₹999 a month "fair" or "cheap" | After 14 Nov |
| H10 | Karigars need credit and would share their record | Credit schemes for goldsmiths exist, but lenders can't see work delivered | ≥ 5 of 10 karigars | After 14 Nov |

**Kill and pivot rules**
- **H1 and H3 both fail:** there is no problem, or no gap. Stop and rethink the problem.
- **H4 fails** (karigars refuse, or are afraid): keep the jeweller-side fine-gold ledger and the deadline agent, make the karigar side optional, and test again.
- **H9 fails but H1–H4 pass:** the problem is real but the buyer is wrong. Probe wholesalers, manufacturers, insurers and associations as payers.

## What we will collect, with consent
- **Scale photos:** at least 300 across at least 10 scale models, with a non-gold object on the pan. This is our accuracy benchmark for reading weights.
- **Voice samples:** at least 150 from at least 20 speakers, in Bengali, Hindi and at least one southern language, under a separate, explicit consent.
- **Trade words:** local terms for wastage, purity, labour and pieces.
- **Pilot commitments:** we will ask for **3 jewellers and 10 karigars** to run a two-week shadow pilot in December, alongside their notebooks.

## How the results will be reported
- A count against each hypothesis, and medians with ranges.
- Three to five anonymised quotes, used with consent.
- The design changes the results caused.
- Raw notes and recordings stay private.
